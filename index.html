05 / Research

Peer-reviewed.Field-tested.

AER // APPLIED ENVIRONMENTAL RESEARCH · VOL. 48, NO. 2 · 2026 · FIRST AUTHOR

Blue carbon stock assessment and climate resilience potential of mangrove forest in Hung Thang commune, Vietnam

Hla Phong Ko, Pham Hong Tinh, Nguyen Thuy Duong

Combines field inventory, soil organic carbon analysis and satellite-based vegetation models to measure carbon stored in Hai Phong's mangroves. Soil organic carbon made up about 19% of the total, and carbon stocks rose steadily from 1996 to 2024.

How I think aboutsustainability as business.

EPR

EPR is a supply-chain project, not a compliance form

Producers that map packaging flows and lock in recycler partnerships early turn EPR fees into recovered value.

GHG ACCOUNTING

Good carbon data starts at the waste bin

Waste audits give companies hard activity data for Scope 3 and expose losses that cost money today.

Traceability builds trust

Digital tracking of materials and emissions lets buyers, regulators and investors verify claims instead of taking them on faith.
