About

Connecting climate ambition with business value.

I am an ESG and circular economy consultant working across Thailand, Myanmar, Vietnam and the wider ASEAN region. My work sits where environmental compliance, digital innovation and commercial partnerships meet: waste audits, GHG accounting, EPR traceability platforms, and the partnerships that make them stick.

Previously Business Development Director and Technical Lead Advisor at RecyGlo, where I led business development, client advisory and regional partnerships, from multi-store audits for global brands to blockchain-enabled recycling traceability.

My path started at sea, with a degree in Nautical Science, and moved into climate science. That mix of operational discipline and research rigour shapes how I work: auditable numbers, practical delivery.

Publications

Peer-reviewed research

Applied Environmental Research · Vol. 48, No. 2 · 2026First author

Blue carbon stock assessment and climate resilience potential of mangrove forest in Hung Thang commune, Vietnam

Hla Phong Ko, Pham Hong Tinh, Nguyen Thuy Duong

Combines field inventory, soil organic carbon analysis and satellite-based vegetation models to measure carbon stored in mangroves in Hai Phong Province. Mangroves held an average of 87.42 tC/ha, with soil organic carbon about 19% of the total, and carbon stocks rose steadily from 1996 to 2024.

Read the article DOI 10.35762/AER.2026012

Insights

Perspectives on sustainability as business

EPR

EPR is a supply-chain project, not a compliance form

Producers that map packaging flows and lock in recycler partnerships early turn EPR fees into recovered value.

GHG accounting

Good carbon data starts at the waste bin

Waste audits give companies hard activity data for Scope 3 and expose losses that cost money today.

Digital MRV

Traceability builds trust

Digital tracking of materials and emissions lets buyers, regulators and investors verify claims instead of taking them on faith.
